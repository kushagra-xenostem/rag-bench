Northwind Labs Travel and Expense Policy (2026)
Fictional sample document for demonstrating RAG Chat Hub.

Flights
Economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer.
All flights must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible.

Hotels
The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in tier-1 cities (for example London, New York, Singapore) and 120 USD elsewhere.
Bookings above the limit require approval from the finance team before the stay.

Meals and Per Diem
Employees travelling on business receive a daily meal allowance of 60 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the end of the trip.
Claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll after approval.
