# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (2026)

*Fictional sample document for demonstrating RAG Chat Hub.*

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their 90-day probation period.
Remote days must be requested in the HR portal and approved by the direct manager at least 48 hours in advance.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.
Working from another country for more than 14 days per calendar year requires written approval from the Legal team.

## Home Office Stipend

Northwind Labs provides a one-time home office stipend of 600 USD.
The stipend is reimbursed against itemised receipts submitted within 60 days of purchase.
Eligible items include a desk, an ergonomic chair, a monitor, and a headset.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.
Public holidays are granted in addition to paid time off and follow the employee's country of employment.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books, and conference tickets.
Requests above 300 USD require manager approval before purchase.
