# Aurora X2 Smart Air Purifier - User Manual

*Fictional sample document for demonstrating RAG Chat Hub.*

## 1. Safety

Place the purifier on a flat, stable surface at least 30 cm away from walls and furniture.
Do not use the device in bathrooms or any room where humidity regularly exceeds 85 percent.
Keep the air inlet clear; blocking it causes the motor to overheat and the unit to shut down automatically.
Unplug the purifier before cleaning, replacing the filter, or moving it to another room.

## 2. Technical Specifications

The Aurora X2 covers rooms up to 45 square metres and delivers a clean air delivery rate (CADR) of 320 cubic metres per hour.
Power consumption ranges from 4 W in Sleep mode to 38 W at Turbo speed.
Noise level is 22 dB in Sleep mode and 52 dB at Turbo speed.
The unit weighs 5.4 kg and measures 24 x 24 x 58 cm.
It connects to the Aurora Home app over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; 5 GHz networks are not supported.

## 3. Filters

The X2 uses a three-stage filter: a washable pre-filter, a H13 HEPA layer and an activated carbon layer.
The HEPA layer captures 99.97 percent of particles as small as 0.3 microns.
The pre-filter should be vacuumed or rinsed with water every two weeks and dried completely before reinstalling.
Replace the combined HEPA and carbon filter every 12 months, or sooner in homes with pets or smokers.
The filter life indicator turns orange when 10 percent of filter life remains and red when the filter must be replaced.
After installing a new filter, hold the Filter button for 5 seconds until the indicator turns white to reset the timer.

## 4. Operating Modes

Auto mode adjusts fan speed using the built-in PM2.5 laser sensor.
Sleep mode dims the display, turns off the indicator lights and runs the fan at its lowest speed.
Turbo mode runs the fan at maximum speed for 30 minutes and then returns to Auto mode.
The child lock is enabled by holding the Lock and Timer buttons together for 3 seconds; repeat to disable it.

## 5. Air Quality Indicator

The ring light shows current air quality: blue means good (PM2.5 below 12), yellow means moderate (12 to 35),
orange means unhealthy for sensitive groups (35 to 55) and red means unhealthy (above 55).
The sensor needs about 3 minutes after power-on to stabilise; readings in that period may be inaccurate.

## 6. Troubleshooting

If the Wi-Fi light blinks quickly, the purifier is in pairing mode; open the Aurora Home app and choose Add Device.
If pairing fails, hold the Wi-Fi button for 10 seconds to reset network settings and try again on a 2.4 GHz network.
A clicking noise usually means the filter is not seated correctly; remove it and reinstall it with the pull tab facing outward.
If the air quality ring stays red in clean air, clean the sensor lens on the back panel with a dry cotton swab.
Error code E1 indicates a fan motor fault; unplug the unit for 1 minute and restart it. If E1 returns, contact support.
Error code E2 indicates the filter is missing or the back cover is not closed.

## 7. Warranty and Support

The Aurora X2 includes a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase, extendable to 3 years by registering the product within 30 days.
The warranty does not cover filters, damage from water exposure, or units opened by unauthorised repairers.
Support is available by email at support@aurora.example and by phone on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00.
